# Voiceover script

First person, Phil Dave's voice. Calm, direct, a little dry. Roughly 150 words a
minute. Style rules from `POSITIONING.md` apply: no invented numbers, claim only
what I do, never the client's wins.

Record each scene as its own file in `vo/` with the file name below. Leave about
half a second of silence at the start and end of each. Once the files exist, the
scenes get retimed to fit the voice (the video may grow to around 2:10), and the
sound effects are pulled down under the speech.

## Sync points worth hitting

- Each "Not a match" in scene 3 lands on a NOT A MATCH stamp.
- "seventy-eight percent" lands as the 78% counter finishes.
- "I focus on finding them" lands on the gold line.
- "seventeen thousand five hundred" lands as the 17,500+ counter finishes.

## Recording tips

- A phone voice memo in a quiet room with soft furnishings is fine. Hold the
  phone about a hand-width from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
- Do two or three takes of each line and keep the best one; mistakes cost
  nothing.
- WAV or M4A both work.
